What gets harder as responsibility grows

You may be leading a larger team or portfolio, moving from technical or clinical expertise into broader leadership, or making decisions with greater consequences.

  • Lead larger teams, programs, or portfolios
  • Set priorities when everything feels important
  • Delegate effectively and help others take ownership
  • Handle difficult relationships and conversations
  • Make decisions when the right path is not obvious
  • Wonder whether you are really ready for a new or expanded role, even when you have the experience and expertise that got you there

Whether you are stepping into a larger role or already leading at a senior level, the challenge is often not simply knowing more. It is deciding what deserves your attention, seeing what you may be missing, and determining how you want to move forward.

What can change

Coaching can help you decide what deserves your attention, prepare for a difficult conversation, determine what to delegate, and make a decision you can act on. The work is aimed at helping you move forward with stronger judgment and a clearer next step.
