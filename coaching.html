Leadership gets harder as responsibility grows.

Coaching gives you a confidential place to work through difficult decisions, relationships, and priorities with someone outside the situation.

Whether you are stepping into broader responsibility or already leading at a senior level, you may be surrounded by people and still have no one you can speak with candidly about a difficult issue.

If the issue involves your boss, your peers, your team, or an important decision, the people closest to you may also be part of the situation.

Stepping into a new or expanded role can also make you wonder whether you are really ready for it, even when you have the experience and expertise that got you there. You may feel pressure to prove yourself quickly to the people who selected you, the people you now lead, and your peers. Some clients describe this as imposter syndrome.

One-to-One Coaching

6 sessions over approximately 3–4 months

We typically meet about every other week.

The engagement includes:

  • Six confidential 50–60 minute virtual coaching sessions
  • Structured reflection between sessions
  • Support and accountability for commitments you choose
  • Brief email support between sessions

Time between sessions gives you an opportunity to try new approaches in your work, see what happens, and bring the experience back into coaching.

Organization-sponsored engagements that require additional sponsor meetings, assessments, reporting, or other services are scoped separately.

What working with me is like

You bring the issues that matter most to you.

I listen carefully, ask direct questions, and challenge your thinking when it is useful.

I bring experience and perspective, but the decisions remain yours. The work is focused on helping you see the situation more clearly, consider options you may not have seen, and determine how you want to move forward.

Experience I bring to the coaching relationship

I have been in leadership roles where there was no easy person to talk to.

My background in nursing, public health, science, and senior leadership helps me understand many of the pressures leaders bring to coaching. I do not assume my experience is the same as yours.
